Remote Shutter

Put one phone where the photo is. Frame and shoot from the other.

  1. Open this page on both phones, on the same Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot.
  2. Scan the camera phone's code with the viewer phone.
  3. Scan the viewer's reply with the camera phone. Done.

Video and photos go straight between your phones. Nothing is uploaded.
