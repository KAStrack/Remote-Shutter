Remote Shutter

Put one phone where the photo is. Frame and shoot from the other.

Put both phones on the same Wi-Fi, or one on the other's hotspot. Then:

  1. On the camera phone, choose This phone is the camera. Scan its code with the other phone.
  2. When the other phone shows its own code, tap Next on the camera phone.
  3. Point the camera phone at that code. You're connected.
